NEXUS GLOBAL ENTERPRISES - EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK & POLICIES (2026 EDITION)

SECTION 1: WORKPLACE HOURS & REMOTE WORK
Nexus operates on a flexible core hours model. Core collaboration hours are between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM in the employee's local time zone. 
Full-time employees are eligible for remote work up to 3 days per week upon completing their 90-day onboarding period.
A home office stipend of $750 is provided annually for remote workstation ergonomics and equipment.

SECTION 2: PAID TIME OFF (PTO) & HOLIDAYS
- Annual Vacation: All full-time employees accrue 22 days of paid vacation per calendar year.
- Sick Leave: 10 days of paid sick leave per year, which does not roll over to subsequent years.
- Parental Leave: Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid leave. Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks.
- Public Holidays: 11 company-wide paid holidays observed annually.

SECTION 3: PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT & LEARNING
Employees can claim up to $2,500 per year for conferences, certifications, books, and courses relevant to their career growth.
Approval must be submitted via the Internal Portal at least two weeks before course registration.

SECTION 4: HEALTHCARE & WELLNESS BENEFITS
Nexus covers 90% of employee healthcare premiums and 75% for dependents.
Coverage includes comprehensive medical, dental, and vision insurance starting on the first day of employment.
A monthly gym/wellness reimbursement of $100 is automatically credited via payroll.
